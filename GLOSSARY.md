# Project Management

This context describes how small software and product teams coordinate work across shared projects.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A shared boundary that contains members and projects.
_Avoid_: Organization, team space

**Project**:
A coordinated body of work within a workspace.

**Task**:
An individual work item in a project, assigned to an owner and optionally given a due date and workflow status.
_Avoid_: Issue, ticket

**Guest account**:
A temporary, email-verified account that lets a visitor try the product with seeded data and usage limits. A guest can become a full account without losing project data.
_Avoid_: Trial user

**Browser sandbox**:
An anonymous, local-only experience for trying the product without creating an account.
_Avoid_: Browser-sandbox guest, local guest account
